Institutional Legacy

Bridging Academic Science & Rural Society

IRDE was founded by agricultural economist Dr. V. V. Ghanekar, inspired by policy icons Dr. Dhananjayrao Gadgil, Dr. M. R. Dhekane, and Dr. P. V. Sukhatme to empower local villages.

In proud partnership with the Extra Mural Studies (Bahishal Shikshan Mandal) of Savitribai Phule Pune University, we carry out intensive educational series, senior citizen advisory camps, and rural youth shibirs.

Pune University Lecture Series

Annual 3-day lecture courses conducted in rural blocks in honor of late leaders Yeshwantrao Chavan, Dr. Babasaheb Jaykar, and Sant Gadgebaba, ensuring vital local wisdom dissemination.

Interactive Guidance Shibirs

Specialized one-day interactive shibirs focusing on personal growth, legal rights, agricultural tools, and psychological counseling for senior citizens and local youth.

Core Mandate

Vision, Mission & Aims

A detailed overview of our ultimate objectives, primary focuses, and the 26 guidelines directing our work across Indian villages.

Our Vision

To uplift the rural communities of India and the globe by designing facilities for health, practical education, and economic growth. Utilizing local resources, rural wisdom, and modern scientific findings, we aim to secure local employment, stabilize rural out-migration, and foster reverse migration.

Helping rural youth develop independent capabilities to solve local challenges.

Our Mission

To prioritize soil testing, crop yield optimization, water conservation, and product marketing. We encourage rural industrialization to ease agricultural labor, introduce organic farming techniques, and establish stable agribusiness systems to safeguard farmers' livelihoods.
