Who We Are

Bridging Scientific Research and Grassroots Rural Development

The Institute of Rural Development and Education (IRDE) is a non-governmental organization founded in 1980 by the visionary economist Dr. V. V. Ghanekar (M.Com., Ph.D.) under the guidance of late visionaries Dr. Dhananjayrao Gadgil, Dr. M. R. Dhekane, and Dr. P. V. Sukhatme.

Officially registered on July 6, 1982, under the Bombay Public Trust Act 1950 (Reg No. F2141 Pune), IRDE has been driving sustainable growth in rural India for over four decades.

We maintain a deep, 15-year partnership with the Extra Mural Studies (Bahishal Shikshan Mandal) of Savitribai Phule Pune University (SPPU), bringing practical, non-formal distance education directly to rural students, farmers, senior citizens, and women.

"To improve the life of rural masses of India and the globe by ensuring facilities for health, education, economic, and social conditions."

— Foundations of the IRDE Mandate

SPPU Bahishal Lecture Series

We organize annual three-day educational lecture series in honor of Yeshwantrao Chavan, Dr. Babasaheb Jaykar, and Sant Gadgebaba, ensuring vital knowledge spreads directly in rural areas.

Interactive Community Shibirs

Active one-day workshops designed for school/college youths and senior citizens, training over 50+ participants per cohort in practical life skills, laws, and health.

Timeline

A Legacy of Rural Development

Key institutional milestones that define our commitment to rural communities.

1980

Foundation & Vision

Founded by Dr. V. V. Ghanekar under the intellectual guidance of late Dr. Dhananjayrao Gadgil, Dr. M. R. Dhekane, and Dr. P. V. Sukhatme, launching initial community surveys in Maharashtra.

1982

Public Trust Registration

Officially registered vide No. F2141 (Pune) on July 6, 1982 under the Bombay Public Trust Act 1950, securing structural legitimacy as a dedicated NGO.

2011

SPPU Bahishal Affiliation

Forged a structural affiliation with Savitribai Phule Pune University's Extra Mural Studies (Bahishal Shikshan Mandal), establishing rural distance learning channels.

2016

Board Expansion

Strengthened the executive committee by appointing senior educational leaders, retired IAS officers, and general managers from the banking sector to drive governance.

Present

Modern Initiatives

Actively operating the Soya Productivity Enhancer Farmers' Program, managing regional lecture camps, and distributing legal and health toolkits to senior citizens.
