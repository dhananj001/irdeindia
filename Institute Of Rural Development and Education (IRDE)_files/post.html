The Institute of Rural Development and Education, Pune organized a program on 27 th January 23 for all the senior citizens of West End Village and other senior citizens of the Right BHusari Colony, Kothrud, Pune. The objective of the program was to educate the senior citizens to write their WILL and Last Testament to avoid the confusion amongst their children's
.Advocate Shri Baburao Hangrale was the chief guest and Shri Vinod Bapat, Senior Advocate was the Chairman of the ...Program. Prof, Dr M C Varshneya, President, IRDE, Pune and Shri Hemant Narute, President, West End Village Apex were the important dignitaries present on the dais. Dr K T Galande, Shri Girish Chitnis, Shri Shripad Nadgonde, Shri P T Jagdale and Shri Dandekar were other important office bearers of IRDE, Pune. The program was compered by Shri Hemant Dandegaonkar Shri S D Gandhare, Senior most Resident of WEV proposed the vote of thanks.
Shri Hangarale said that Preparing a WILL is a very easy task which can be prepared hand written or typed and either notorized or registered in The Court. Shri Bapat said that you can change it if need arise but it should be mentioned in your draft that this Will supersedes all the other will written. by me.
People raised lot of queries which were very sportively answered by the speaker.
